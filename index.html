Notes

  • Clip enumeration. HC and LC contiguous subsequences are generated as candidate clips, controlled by Max cuts per chain: 0 = full chain only · 1 = terminal fragments (Fab, Fc/2, etc.) · 2 = include internal fragments. Min clip length defaults to 50 aa (≈ 5.5 kDa) since intact mass deconvolution typically resolves ≥ 10 kDa species; lower it for smaller fragments or raise it (e.g. 100 aa ≈ 11 kDa) to be stricter.
  • Non-reduced mode. IgG is held together by inter-chain S–S, so the tool searches assemblies of 1–4 chains (≤ 2 HC + 2 LC) using the auto-detected disulfide topology: CPPC motif defines hinge HH bonds; last Cys before the hinge in HC and last Cys in LC define the HL bond; remaining Cys are paired sequentially as intra-chain bonds. Each assembly's S–S count is computed exactly from the Cys positions actually present, and assemblies not physically connected by inter-chain S–S (e.g., 1 HC + 2 LC, or 2 LC alone) are rejected.
  • Reduced mode. No S–S adjustment; only single-clip search runs since each chain floats free.
  • Modifications. Glycoforms apply at any N-X-S/T (X≠P) sequon inside a clip. Pyro-Glu requires the clip to start at HC/LC position 1 with Q or E. C-terminal Lys clipping applies only when an HC clip ends at the chain's C-terminus on K. Cysteinylation (+119.14 Da) and Glutathionylation (+305.31 Da) are S–S adducts that require the clip to contain at least one Cys; they're optional (off by default) since most CHO-produced IgGs are fully oxidized.
  • Scoring. Canonical species (Full mAb, Half-mAb, Fab, F(ab')₂, Fc, Fc/2, HC, LC) get a class bonus. Cleavages at chemical-lability hotspots earn additional bonuses by category — Asp-mediated (D–P most labile, then D–G, D–S/T, generic D–X, X–D); Asn-mediated (N–G, N–S); hinge/flexible-region motifs (G–G, G–L, L–G, S–G, A–S, L–S); Cys-adjacent (X–C from radical-mediated chemistry). Cuts at none of these hotspots get a parsimony penalty.
  • Enzyme-specific bonuses. Papain (~HC:224), IdeS (~HC:236), pepsin (~HC:234), and GingisKHAN (~HC:238) only contribute to the score when the corresponding Enzyme treatment is selected. For untreated CHO drug substance, none of these apply.
  • IgG1 topology only. The auto-detected disulfide topology assumes a canonical IgG1 architecture (CPPC hinge motif → 2 HH bonds, last Cys before hinge ↔ last Cys in LC → 1 HL bond, remaining Cys paired sequentially as intra-chain). For IgG2, IgG3, IgG4, Fabs, scFvs, bispecifics, or fusion proteins the detected topology will likely be incorrect and assembly masses will be off. Verify the topology summary printed below the sequence textareas before trusting any multi-chain results.
  • Use the matcher for orientation, not validation. Confirm proposed clips by peptide mapping or middle-down sequencing before publishing. Provided as-is, no warranty — not for regulatory submissions or batch release decisions without orthogonal confirmation.
